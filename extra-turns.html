🌀 Why Extra Turns Are Banned in Portal

Extra Turns

Core Reason

Extra turns are banned because they break the rhythm of Surges and give one player multiple opportunities to dominate combat, Glitch, and King/Queen in a row.

Design Philosophy

Portal is built around:

  • Surges — one turn per player per cycle.
  • Shared pacing — everyone gets equal chances.
  • King or Queen of the Surge — evaluated once per Surge.

Extra turns:

  • Let one player take multiple Surges back-to-back.
  • Double or triple their access to Glitch and King/Queen.
  • Create huge tempo swings that feel unfair.

Gameplay Problems

  • Snowballing advantage — one extra turn often leads to another.
  • Table frustration — everyone else waits while one player chains turns.
  • Rule complexity — Glitch timing and King/Queen evaluation become messy.

Practical Rule

Any card that grants an additional turn after the current one is banned from Portal.
