👑 Why "You Win / You Lose" Cards Are Banned in Portal

"You Win / You Lose" Cards

Core Reason

"You win the game" or "you lose the game" cards are banned because they bypass combat, Surges, Glitch, and King/Queen, skipping the core gameplay Portal is built around.

Design Philosophy

Portal's core loop:

  • Draw from a shared deck.
  • Play creatures and spells.
  • Fight in combat.
  • Manipulate exile and the Portal.
  • Earn King or Queen of the Surge.

Auto-win/lose cards:

  • Ignore combat.
  • Ignore board state.
  • Ignore Glitch and exile.
  • Turn the game into "assemble condition, press button."

Gameplay Problems

  • Non-combat victories — winning without interacting on the battlefield.
  • Deck warping — decks built entirely around one card.
  • Anti-climax — the game ends on a rules text line, not a dramatic board state.

Practical Rule

Any card whose text includes "you win the game" or "you lose the game" as a primary effect or condition is banned from Portal.
