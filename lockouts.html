🔥 Why Lockout Cards Are Banned in Portal

Oppressive Lockouts

Core Reason

Lockout cards are banned because they prevent players from participating, which is the opposite of Portal's chaotic, shared-experience design.

Design Philosophy

Portal wants:

  • Everyone drawing.
  • Everyone casting.
  • Everyone attacking and blocking.
  • Everyone affected by Glitch and King/Queen.

Lockout cards:

  • Stop players from casting spells.
  • Stop players from drawing cards.
  • Stop players from using their mana.
  • Turn the game into "watch one player play."

Gameplay Problems

  • Non-games — players sit on dead hands.
  • Frustration — the format feels oppressive instead of wild.
  • Design conflict — Glitch and King/Queen can't shine if no one can act.

Practical Rule

Any card that significantly restricts players from casting spells, drawing cards, or using their resources on a recurring basis is banned from Portal.
