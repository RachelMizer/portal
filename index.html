Welcome to the Portal

PORTAL is a multiplayer, shared-deck, creature-slot combat format for Magic: The Gathering. One 150-card deck sits at the center of the table. Every player draws from it, exiles from it, and battles for control of three creature slots while the Portal itself destabilizes reality through unpredictable Glitch effects.

It's fast, dramatic, and a little chaotic — every Surge reshapes the battlefield, and no two games unfold the same way.

New to Portal? Start with the Core Rules to learn how Surges, slots, and the Portal Glitch work, then check the Banned List before you sit down to build or borrow a deck.
