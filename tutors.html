🔮 Why Tutors Are Banned in Portal

Tutors

Core Reason

Tutors are banned because they break the shared-deck chaos and let players cherry-pick the best cards, undermining Portal's "embrace what the deck gives you" identity.

Design Philosophy

Portal wants:

  • Uncertainty — you don't know what's coming.
  • Shared randomness — everyone draws from the same pool.
  • Exile tension — good cards can vanish into exile.

Tutors:

  • Turn randomness into certainty.
  • Let players fetch bombs on demand.
  • Reduce the importance of Surges and Glitch.

Gameplay Problems

  • Consistency over chaos — decks become "tutor for X" instead of "adapt to what you draw."
  • Power spikes — repeated access to the same bomb.
  • King/Queen distortion — the player with tutors can engineer perfect turns.

Practical Rule

Any card that searches your library for a specific card or card type and puts it into your hand, on top of your deck, or directly onto the battlefield is banned from Portal.
