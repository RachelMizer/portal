🧬 Why Permanent Control Effects Are Banned in Portal

Permanent Control Effects

Core Reason

Permanent control effects are banned because they break slot identity and token anchoring, which are central to Portal's battlefield design.

Design Philosophy

Portal's battlefield:

  • 3 creature slots per player.
  • Slot 1 = Creator Slot (special rules).
  • Tokens tied to their parent's slot.
  • Replacement and death tied to slots.

Permanent control:

  • Steals creatures without moving slots.
  • Creates confusion about ownership and tokens.
  • Breaks the clean "your slots, your board" model.

Gameplay Problems

  • Board confusion — whose creature is in which slot?
  • Token weirdness — who controls tokens tied to stolen parents?
  • Rule headaches — replacement, death, and Glitch interactions get messy.

Practical Rule

Any card that permanently gains control of an opponent's creature (without clearly fitting Portal's slot and token rules) is banned from Portal.
