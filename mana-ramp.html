🧨 Why Cost Reduction and Mana Engines Are Banned in Portal

Mana Ramp / Cost Reduction Engines

Core Reason

Cost reduction and mana engines are banned because they undermine the Mana Drain mechanic and Portal's intended pacing.

Design Philosophy

Portal's mana:

  • Is tracked per player.
  • Can be reorganized via Mana Drain.
  • Is meant to feel like a resource you manage deliberately.

Cost reduction and mana engines:

  • Make spells effectively free.
  • Create huge tempo spikes.
  • Allow players to ignore Mana Drain and resource tension.

Gameplay Problems

  • Explosive turns — casting far more than intended.
  • Resource trivialization — mana stops mattering.
  • Design conflict — Mana Drain becomes irrelevant.

Practical Rule

Cards that significantly reduce the cost of multiple spells or generate large amounts of repeatable mana are banned from Portal.
