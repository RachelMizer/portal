💥 Why Infinite Combos Are Banned in Portal

Infinite Combo Enablers

Core Reason

Infinite combos are banned because they collapse the game into a single non-interactive moment, which goes against Portal's core design: chaotic, shared-deck combat where every Surge matters.

Design Philosophy

Portal is built around:

  • Shared fate — everyone draws from the same deck.
  • Surges — short, punchy turns.
  • Exile and Glitch — long-term chaos and manipulation.
  • King or Queen of the Surge — rewarding aggression and board presence.

Infinite combos:

  • Skip Surges.
  • Ignore Glitch.
  • Ignore King/Queen.
  • Turn the game into "assemble the combo and win."

Gameplay Problems

  • Non-interactive wins — one player wins without meaningful response.
  • Deck warping — decks become "find the combo" instead of "play the game."
  • Bad feels — everyone else watches one player solitaire.

Practical Rule

Any card or combination of cards that can generate infinite damage, infinite mana, infinite tokens, or infinite life is banned from Portal.
