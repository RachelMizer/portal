💥 Infinite Combo Enablers

❓ Why infinite combos are banned

  • Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker
  • Splinter Twin
  • Heliod, Sun-Crowned
  • Walking Ballista
  • Devoted Druid
  • Vizier of Remedies
  • Basalt Monolith
  • Grim Monolith
  • Peregrine Drake
  • Palinchron

Anything that creates infinite loops is gone.

🔮 Tutors

❓ Why tutors are banned

  • Demonic Tutor
  • Vampiric Tutor
  • Enlightened Tutor
  • Mystical Tutor
  • Worldly Tutor
  • Idyllic Tutor
  • Eladamri's Call
  • Green Sun's Zenith
  • Chord of Calling
  • Birthing Pod

Tutors break shared-deck chaos and let players cherry-pick bombs.

🌀 Extra Turns

❓ Why extra turns are banned

  • Time Warp
  • Temporal Manipulation
  • Time Stretch
  • Nexus of Fate
  • Walk the Aeons
  • Capture of Jingzhou

No one gets two Surges in a row.

👑 "You Win / You Lose" Cards

❓ Why auto-win cards are banned

  • Approach of the Second Sun
  • Felidar Sovereign
  • Biovisionary
  • Thassa's Oracle
  • Laboratory Maniac
  • Jace, Wielder of Mysteries

Portal is a battlefield game — not a "surprise, I win" game.

❓ Why lockout cards are banned

  • Winter Orb
  • Static Orb
  • Stasis
  • Blood Moon
  • Back to Basics
  • Rule of Law
  • Arcane Laboratory
  • Narset, Parter of Veils

🧬 Permanent Control Effects

❓ Why control cards are banned

  • Mind Control
  • Control Magic
  • Treachery
  • Bribery
  • Gilded Drake
  • Agent of Treachery
  • Sower of Temptation

🌋 Excessive Token Engines

❓ Why token engines are banned

  • Avenger of Zendikar
  • Hornet Queen
  • Secure the Wastes
  • Entreat the Angels
  • Parallel Lives
  • Doubling Season
  • Anointed Procession

Token-creator creatures already force Slot 1 shifts — these would break the system.

🧨 Mana Ramp / Cost Reduction Engines

❓ Why cost-reduction is banned

  • Nykthos, Shrine to Nyx
  • Cabal Coffers
  • Crypt Ghast
  • High Tide
  • Cloud Key
  • Herald's Horn
  • Urza's Incubator
  • Training Grounds

Mana manipulation must come from Mana Drain, not broken engines.
